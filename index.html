Putting the pieces together with JobLedger

Building JobLedger gave me hands-on practice connecting a React and Next.js interface to Supabase authentication and a PostgreSQL database. I worked through company workspaces, owner and employee access, inventory transactions, job costs, CSV exports, and deployment to a custom domain. I also built a public demo so visitors can explore the application without an account.
